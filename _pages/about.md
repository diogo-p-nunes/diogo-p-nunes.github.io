*NLP/ML Researcher & Engineer @ [INESC-ID Lisbon](https://www.inesc-id.pt/)/[Human Language Technologies lab](https://www.hlt.inesc-id.pt/w/Diogo_A.P._Nunes)*  
*PhD Candidate @ [Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/en/)*  
(Thesis defense expected by Oct/Nov 2025)  
**Contact:** [diogoafonsopedronunes@gmail.com](mailto:diogoafonsopedronunes@gmail.com)

I'm a NLP/ML researcher & engineer. In the context of my PhD, I’ve been exploring how language relates to psychological and clinical states, leveraging computational models for chronic pain assessment (clinical state, placebo effect, depression, anxiety, etc.). In 2024, I was a visiting researcher @ [Feinberg School of Medicine - Northwestern University](https://www.feinberg.northwestern.edu/index.html), in Chicago (US). This research was in collaboration with [Faculty of Medicine of the University of Porto (FMUP)](https://sigarra.up.pt/fmup/pt/web_page.inicial) and [São João Hospital](https://portal-chsj.min-saude.pt/) in Porto. We have published nationally and internationally. Part of our work was awarded the ["Best Clinical Research Paper Award 2021"](https://www.aped-dor.org/index.php/premios/revista-dor-bene) by the [Portuguese Association for the Study of Pain](https://www.aped-dor.org/). Our research has also been discussed in the press ([Sábado](https://fisioglobal.pt/as-novas-armas-contra-a-dor-cronica-revista-sabado/), [Público](https://www.publico.pt/2023/04/22/ciencia/noticia/garantidamente-ja-sentimos-dor-fisica-2046798)) and selected as a [finalist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GXs4pEkG8Kk&ab_channel=ULisboa) for the ["3 Minute Thesis"](https://www.ulisboa.pt/en/info/three-minute-thesis-university-lisbon) competition in 2023. I have been an invited lecturer @ [IST](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/en/) and [ISCTE](https://www.iscte-iul.pt/). I'll be a keynote speaker @ [International Conference on Computational Pain Neuroscience](https://events.au.dk/computationalpainneuroscienceconference2025) in [Aarhus University](https://cfin.au.dk/), Denmark.

**My recent work includes:**
- For placebo effect prediction: LLM continual pretraining with web-scraped text data for domain adaptation. Predictive modeling based on LLM encoding, SVM and Decision Tree models. Text mining and clustering, coupled with LLM prompting, for interpretability.
- For depression symptom detection: RAG, LLM fine-tuning, data synthesis, and other Information Retrieval (IR) techniques.
- For clinical state prediction: Psycholinguistic feature extraction (LIWC/SEANCE). Predictive modeling. Error analysis.
- For social media analysis: Large-scale web scraping, text mining, topic modeling, and clustering. Graph analysis.

**Tools I have worked with:** PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, vLLM, Ollama, Git, LangChain, LangGraph, NLTK, SpaCy, Pandas, NumPy
